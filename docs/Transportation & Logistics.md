# **3\. REPLACE “WHAT BREAKS ON THE PLANT FLOOR”**

**Eyebrow**  
`WHERE LOGISTICS BREAKS`

### **What creates friction in transportation & logistics.**

As shipment volume, customers, drivers, locations, and delivery requirements grow, disconnected systems make operations harder to coordinate.

### **01 — Disconnected dispatch**

Jobs, drivers, vehicles, and schedules sit across different tools, making day-to-day planning difficult.

### **02 — Limited shipment visibility**

Teams spend time chasing updates when shipment status and location aren't readily available.

### **03 — Manual job allocation**

Dispatchers repeatedly match jobs, capacity, drivers, vehicles, and delivery requirements by hand.

### **04 — Inefficient route planning**

Poorly coordinated routes can increase travel time, operational effort, and unnecessary mileage.

### **05 — Delivery exceptions**

Delays, failed deliveries, address issues, and other exceptions become difficult to manage without structured workflows.
