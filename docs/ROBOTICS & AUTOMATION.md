# **3\. “WHAT SLOWS AUTOMATION TEAMS”**

**Eyebrow**  
`WHERE AUTOMATION BREAKS`

### **What creates friction in automated operations.**

Automation becomes harder to manage when machines, software, workflows, and operational data evolve independently.

### **01 — Disconnected systems**

Robots, machines, sensors, and business applications operate separately instead of sharing information through connected workflows.

### **02 — Noisy alerts**

Too many unstructured alerts make it difficult for operators to identify what actually requires attention.

### **03 — Manual handoffs**

Automated processes still depend on people transferring information or triggering the next step manually.

### **04 — Fragile integrations**

Point-to-point connections become difficult to maintain as hardware, applications, and operational requirements change.

### **05 — Limited operational visibility**

Without a unified view, teams struggle to understand robot status, workflow progress, faults, and system performance.
