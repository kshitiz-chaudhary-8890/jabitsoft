# Service Card Reference-Match Design

## Scope

Rebuild only the static Services card. Keep the Services heading and every other page section unchanged.

## Composition

- Wide raised white shell with a soft shadow and generous rounded corners.
- Two inset panels separated by a narrow gutter: light panel at roughly 44%, dark panel at roughly 56%.
- Light panel contains the service label, compact two-line headline, description, tags, and one dark CTA aligned toward the bottom.
- Dark panel contains a headline, description, a restrained overlapping-circle capability diagram, and one blue status badge. It has no CTA button.

## Visual System

- Existing website typography only.
- White, neutral gray, near-black, and sparse Jabit Blue (#0099FF).
- Thin borders, restrained shadows, compact pills, and no animation.
- Stack panels vertically on small screens without horizontal overflow.
